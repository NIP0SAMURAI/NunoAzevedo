Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Nuno Azevedo (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you visit this website or interact with our online content.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Contact details you provide by email or through a form.
  • Information you submit when requesting commissions, collaborations, or inquiries.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device, pages visited, and referral source.
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies used to improve site experience and performance.

2. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and messages.
  • Manage communication regarding exhibitions, projects, and commissions.
  • Improve the performance, accessibility, and content of this website.
  • Assess website usage and user experience through aggregated analytics.

3. Legal basis

We process personal data only when there is a valid reason to do so, including your consent, the performance of a contract, legal compliance, or a legitimate interest in operating and improving the website.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with trusted service providers who support website hosting, email communication, analytics, or technical operation, provided they are bound by appropriate confidentiality and data protection obligations.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website may use cookies to remember preferences, understand how users navigate the site, and support basic functionality. For more information, please refer to our Cookie Policy.

6. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Restrict or withdraw consent where consent is used.

If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns, please contact Nuno Azevedo via the contact information available on the website.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any material changes will be reflected on this page with the updated date.
